**Owner:** Brand Strategist
**As of:** 2026-10-17
**Status:** v1. A preliminary, web-based naming screen. **It is not trademark clearance or legal advice.** Nothing here replaces a full search and opinion from a trademark attorney in each filing country (see §6).

**How to read this document**
- **[V]** means verified: I saw it myself on the cited URL or in a live lookup on 2026-10-17. **[U]** means unverified: it comes from a secondary source, an aggregator, inference, or my own linguistic knowledge, and needs confirmation.
- **Domain checks** used live RDAP lookups (`rdap.org`, which follows the IANA bootstrap) on 2026-10-17. "Registered (YYYY)" means a registration record exists, with its creation year. - **App Store checks** used Apple's public iTunes Search API for the IN, AE and SA storefronts. Play Store results come only from web search **[U]**.
- **Linguistic notes** on Arabic, Hindi/Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada are the strategist's own analysis **[U]**. Native-speaker panels must confirm them (§6, step 6).
- Context from sibling docs: launch is **India first (Bengaluru, Hyderabad)**, then **UAE (Dubai)**, then **KSA** (`01-market-competitors-regulation.md`). That order drives the filing order in §6.

## Executive summary

1. **Verdict on "Nabd": replace it as the public company and product brand. Risk: HIGH.** Three things make it unsafe: (a) **Nabd (نبض), the Arabic news aggregator** run by NABD Limited from Dubai since 2012, with nabd.com and claimed 20M+ users, which holds the Class 9 "app" space across the GCC; (b) an **existing medical booking app called "Nabd | نبض: احجز طبيبك"** (ICORESOFT, Feb 2026) that covers doctors, pharmacies, labs and home care, which is our product almost exactly, and is visible in the IN, AE and SA App Stores; (c) other Nabd-branded products launched in 2026: a Jordanian lab app and a B2B attendance SaaS that holds getnabd.com. On top of that, "نبض" (pulse) is a descriptive, crowded word in Arabic health branding, and in India "Nabd" sounds close to **NABH**, the hospital accreditation board our hospital buyers deal with. **"Nabd Health" or "NabdOS" does not fix this,** because "Nabd" stays the dominant element of the mark. Keep "Nabd" as an **internal codename** only.
2. **Recommended names, ranked:**
   1. **Intizam** (انتظام / इंतज़ाम): "order, arrangement, management". The word is the same in Arabic and Hindi-Urdu, and Urdu writes it with **identical Arabic-script spelling**. No healthcare or software conflicts found. **intizam.com is for sale at $2,395** [V], and .health/.ai/.in/.app/.care show no registration record.
   2. **Raahat** (राहत / راحة): "relief, comfort". The warmest patient-facing name, and natural in both languages. **raahat.com is listed "may be for sale"** [V]. The weak point is that "Rahat" is a crowded name among local Indian hospitals, so the mark is weaker and harder to own.
   3. **Nadiyo**: an invented name built on Sanskrit *nāḍī* (pulse), which carries the "pulse" idea of Nabd across languages. The cleanest legally (no conflicts found) and **.com shows no registration record** [V], but it carries less meaning and needs storytelling.
3. **Suggested architecture:** **Intizam Health Technologies** as the company. Products: **Intizam Clinic / Hospital / Pharmacy / Lab**, plus **Intizam Connect** as the patient WhatsApp aggregator. Optional phase 2: the consumer app as **"Raahat by Intizam"**, with "Dawrak" (دورك, "your turn") as the GCC queue alert. Tagline spine: *"Intizam for the clinic. Raahat for the patient."*
4. **Next action:** buy **intizam.com** now, before the name is linked to us publicly, and commission full searches in **India → UAE → KSA** in **Nice Classes 9, 35, 42 and 44, with 38 and 10 recommended**. Filing routes (§6): **India, UAE, KSA, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain are all Madrid Protocol members**. KSA has been one since **8 Oct 2026**. **Kuwait is the only GCC state outside Madrid** and needs a national filing.

## 1. Assessment of "Nabd"

### 1.1 What the word does well
- نبض (*nabḍ*) means "pulse" or "heartbeat". It is short, alive and medically resonant for Arabic speakers. It also has a cross-cultural echo: Hindustani **नब्ज़ / نبض (*nabz*)** is the same word, borrowed from Arabic via Persian ("nabz dekhna" means "to check the pulse").
- These strengths are exactly why the name is crowded.

### 1.4 Linguistic and usability notes [U]
- **Arabic:** positive. ض (*ḍād*) is the "letter of Arabic"; non-Arabs flatten it to *d* or *z*. The word is generic for heart and health campaigns, which makes the mark weak and descriptive for Class 44 in Arabic-speaking examiners' eyes.
- **Hindi-belt:** the final cluster *-bd* is unnatural, so people say "Nabad" or "Nabadh". Spelling drifts (Nabd / Nabad / Nabadh) and voice search suffers. NABH confusion as above.
- **South India:** no known negative meaning in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam or Kannada. The word is simply opaque there.

### 1.5 Risk rating and recommendation

| Dimension | Rating |
|---|---|
| Trademark conflict (GCC, Class 9) | **High.** A dominant prior user with a likely well-known mark (#1), and a direct same-services app (#2) |
| Trademark conflict (Class 44, Arabic markets) | **High.** Descriptive or weak, and crowded |
| Trademark conflict (India) | Medium. No identical mark found, but phonetic proximity to NABH, and the GCC users above are visible on Indian stores |
| Domain | High. Every useful TLD except .health is taken |
| App-store discoverability | **High.** A search for "Nabd" returns the 20M-user news app first in every store |

**Recommendation: Replace.** Do **not** go with "Nabd Health", "NabdOS", "Nabd Care" or "Nabdi". Under GCC and Indian practice, adding a descriptive word does not avoid confusion when "Nabd" stays the distinctive part, and "Nabdi" looks a lot like **Nahdi**, Saudi Arabia's largest pharmacy chain ([V] [Arab News on NahdiCare](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1750396/corporate-news)).
**Keep "Nabd" as the internal codename.** The `com.nabd.hms` package and repo names can stay. However, **change any identifier the public sees before the first store release**: iOS/Android bundle IDs, API hostnames, WhatsApp Business display name, email domains and `localStorage` keys such as `nabd_access_token` that appear in browser dev tools.

## 6. Clearance checklist for the trademark attorney

**The founder must not announce, file a company name, or print anything until steps 1–7 are done for the chosen name.**

1. **Knockout search** (attorney, 1–2 days). Identical and similar marks in **WIPO Global Brand Database**, **IP India public search**, **UAE MoET** trademark e-services ([moet.gov.ae](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/web/guest/w/trademark-services-1)), **SAIP**, **Qatar MoCI**, **Oman MOCIIP**, **Kuwait MoCI**, **Bahrain MOIC**. Search **both Latin and Arabic script** (انتظام, راحة, ناديو) and **phonetic transliterations** (Intezam/Intazam; Rahat/Raha).
2. **Specifically confirm the NABD position:** pull NABD Limited's registrations and classes in the UAE, KSA, Kuwait and Egypt. This documents why we are moving away, and confirms that none of our candidate names sits near them.
3. **Full availability search plus written opinion** for the winner (a vendor like Corsearch/CompuMark with Arabic phonetic coverage). Include common-law/unregistered use: company registries (MCA India, UAE/DED and free zones, KSA MoC/"Maroof"), health facility licensing lists (DHA/DoH/MOHAP, SFDA pharmacy register, NABH directory), app stores and domains.
4. **Classes (Nice, 12th edition).** Confirm the specifications.
   - **9**: downloadable software and mobile apps (clinic/HMS, pharmacy, lab, patient app).
   - **35**: business management for healthcare providers, appointment scheduling for others, online marketplace/aggregator services, retail pharmacy-related services, advertising.
   - **42**: SaaS/PaaS, hosting, software development, electronic health-record platforms.
   - **44**: medical information, telemedicine coordination, pharmacy advice, medical lab services for diagnosis.
   - **Recommended additions:** **38** (telecom/messaging, i.e. WhatsApp-based patient communication and data transmission), **10** (only if we ever ship devices), **36** (only if we process payments or insurance claims; possible in KSA through NPHIES), **5** (only if we launch private-label pharmacy products).
5. **Filing strategy and routes.**
   - **India first** (launch market): file at **IP India** (Trade Marks Act 1999; multi-class applications allowed). Claim **Paris Convention 6-month priority** for the GCC filings.
   - **Madrid Protocol** (WIPO, 117 members covering 133 countries [V] [WIPO members](https://www.wipo.int/madrid/en/members/)) can cover most targets from the Indian base application:
     - **India**: member since 8 Jul 2013 [U, attorney to confirm date]
     - **UAE**: member, in force **28 Dec 2021** [V] [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/intellectual-property/united-arab-emirates-joining-the-madrid-protocol-for-international-trademark-registration). Office: **Ministry of Economy & Tourism (MoET)** under Federal Decree-Law No. 36 of 2021 [V] [MoET](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/-/ministry-of-economy-and-tourism-introduces-trademark-service-fees-as-part-of-efforts-to-enhance-uae-s-appeal-for-national-and-international-brands-and-support-sme-owners)
     - **Saudi Arabia**: member, in force **8 Oct 2026** (accession deposited 8 Jul 2026) [V] [Rouse](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2026/saudi-arabia-joins-the-madrid-system-key-implications-for-trade-mark-owners), [CITMA](https://www.citma.org.uk/resources/saudi-arabia-joins-madrid-system-mb26.html). Office: **SAIP**. The system is brand new in KSA, so ask counsel whether a **direct national SAIP filing** is still faster or safer for now.
     - **Qatar**: member, in force **3 Aug 2024**, with an 18-month refusal period [V] [WIPO news](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/w/news/2024/qatar-joins-the-madrid-system). Office: **MoCI IP Department**.
     - **Oman**: member (2007) [V per Rouse; date U]. **Bahrain**: member (2005) [V per Rouse; date U].
     - **Kuwait: NOT a Madrid member** [V] [Rouse](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2026/saudi-arabia-joins-the-madrid-system-key-implications-for-trade-mark-owners). It needs a **national filing** at the Ministry of Commerce & Industry (expect legalised POA and other formalities).
   - **GCC Unified Trademark Law:** member states apply a common law nationally, but there is **no single GCC trademark registration or office**, so each country needs its own filing (or designation) [U, confirm with counsel]. The law protects **well-known marks** even across classes, which is the main legal reason to stay away from "Nabd".
   - **Madrid caveat:** for 5 years an international registration depends on the Indian base mark ("central attack"). If the Indian filing is opposed, the GCC designations fall with it. Counsel may advise a **UAE or national base** for the GCC instead.
6. **Linguistic and cultural clearance.** Native-speaker panels (5+ each): Gulf Arabic (KSA, UAE), Levantine and Egyptian Arabic (large expat clinician base in the GCC), MSA, Hindi, Urdu, **Kannada and Telugu (Bengaluru/Hyderabad launch)**, Tamil, Malayalam (large Kerala diaspora in the GCC), Bengali and Marathi. Test meaning, slang, religious sensitivity and ease of saying and spelling the name aloud (dictate it on the phone, then ask the listener to type it).
7. **Regulatory naming constraints.** Confirm that the name does not imply a government or licensed-facility status (e.g. "hospital", "pharmacy" or "lab" in a *company* name can trigger licensing questions with DHA/DoH/SFDA/state pharmacy councils). Keep those words as **product descriptors**, not in the legal entity name.
8. **File** word mark (Latin), word mark (Arabic script) and composite logo; or one combined Latin+Arabic mark where the local office prefers it. Watch the opposition windows (India: 4 months after advertisement in the TM Journal; GCC: typically 30–60 days, **verify per country**).
9. **Watch service** on the mark and close variants in all 7 jurisdictions and both app stores.
10. **Housekeeping:** move public-facing identifiers off "nabd" before the first release (bundle IDs, API hosts, email domain, WhatsApp display name, `nabd_*` browser storage keys). Internal code (`com.nabd.hms`) can remain the codename.
